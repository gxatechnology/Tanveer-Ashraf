## 1. Statutory & Institutional Authorities

All 162 topic concept notes, 700 practice MCQs, and 228 historical PYQ mappings in the Phase 6 General Knowledge Learning Academy are anchored directly in authoritative government, statutory, and official academic standards.

### A. History of India (`gk-history`)
- **Primary Curricular Standard:** Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) Judicial Services Examination Syllabus (Paper I: General Knowledge).
- **Core Scholarly References:**
  - Ancient India: NCERT Class XI (Prof. R.S. Sharma); D.N. Jha, *Ancient India in Historical Outline*.
  - Medieval India: NCERT Class XI (Prof. Satish Chandra); *History of Medieval India (800–1700)*.
  - Modern India: Bipan Chandra, Mridula Mukherjee, Aditya Mukherjee, K.N. Panikkar, Sucheta Mahajan, *India's Struggle for Independence*; Rajiv Ahir, *A Brief History of Modern India* (Spectrum Books).
  - Constitutional Milestones: Sir Courtenay Ilbert, *The Government of India*; Keith, *A Constitutional History of India*.

### B. Indian Culture (`gk-culture`)
- **Institutional Authorities:**
  - **Sangeet Natak Akademi (SNA):** National Academy of Music, Dance and Drama official recognition of the eight classical dance traditions (Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Sattriya).
  - **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI):** Monuments of National Importance, architectural surveys of Nagara, Dravida, and Vesara temples, and rock-cut cave monuments (Ajanta, Ellora, Elephanta).
  - **Ministry of Culture, Government of India:** Classical languages criteria (Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia) and National Mission on Manuscripts.
  - **UNESCO World Heritage Centre:** Official register of Indian Tangible and Intangible Cultural Heritage sites.
  - **Art & Literature:** NCERT, *An Introduction to Indian Art* (Part I & II); Kapila Vatsyayan, *Indian Classical Dance*.

### C. Indian Geography (`gk-geography`)
- **Institutional Authorities:**
  - **Survey of India:** Official national cartographic standards, borders, and relief models.
  - **Indian Meteorological Department (IMD):** Monsoon mechanisms, southwest and northeast monsoon tracks, Western Disturbances, and rainfall classifications.
  - **Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR):** Official 8-fold soil classification system of India.
  - **Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC):** National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Tiger Reserves, and Biosphere Reserves data; Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 schedule revisions.
  - **Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India:** Primary Census Abstract (Census 2011) for demographic, sex ratio, density, and literacy indicators.

### D. Indian Polity (`gk-polity`)
- **Statutory & Constitutional Authorities:**
  - **Constitution of India:** Ministry of Law and Justice (Legislative Department), incorporating all amendments up to the 106th Constitutional Amendment Act (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, 2023).
  - **Statutory Enactments:** Representation of the People Act, 1950 & 1951; Delimitation Act, 2002; Right to Information Act, 2005; Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993; Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013; Central Vigilance Commission Act, 2003.
  - **Judicial Precedents:** Supreme Court of India landmark constitutional bench rulings (*Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala*, *Minerva Mills*, *S.R. Bommai*, *Indra Sawhney*, *L. Chandra Kumar*, *K.S. Puttaswamy*).
  - **Commission Reports:** Sarkaria Commission (1988) and M.M. Punchhi Commission (2010) on Center-State Relations; Law Commission of India reports on electoral reforms.

### E. Indian Economy (`gk-economy`)
- **Statutory & Official Authorities:**
  - **Reserve Bank of India (RBI):** RBI Act, 1934; Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) framework; Annual Reports and Master Directions on Banking Regulations and NPA classifications.
  - **Ministry of Finance, Government of India:** Union Budget documents, Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2003; Economic Survey of India.
  - **Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI):** Central Statistics Office (CSO) National Accounts Statistics (Base Year 2011-12); National Statistical Office (NSO) Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS).
  - **Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council:** 101st Constitutional Amendment Act; Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.
  - **NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India):** Strategy for New India, SDG India Index, and Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) reports.
  - **Poverty Estimation Methodology:** Expert Group Reports (Prof. D.T. Lakdawala, Prof. Suresh Tendulkar, Dr. C. Rangarajan).

## 2. Pedagogical Alignment with UP PCS-J Paper I

The syllabus and topic choices are directly aligned with the historical question patterns of the Uttar Pradesh Judicial Services Examination Paper I (General Knowledge):
1. Historical focus places heavy weight on the Indian Freedom Struggle (1857–1947), Socio-Religious movements, and ancient administration.
2. Culture emphasizes classical dance origins, temple architectural terminology (Shikhara, Vimana, Garbhagriha), and Bhakti/Sufi saints.
3. Geography emphasizes river tributaries, multi-purpose dams, national parks, mineral belts, and monsoon dynamics.
4. Polity focuses on constitutional articles, parliamentary privileges, emergency mechanisms, constitutional bodies, and landmark judgments.
5. Economy emphasizes inflation indexes, monetary policy tools, budget deficits, banking terms, and government welfare programs.
