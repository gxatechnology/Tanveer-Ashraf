## 3. Landmark Judicial Decisions & Precedents Cited

| Case Title & Citation | Forum & Year | Primary Legal Principle Established |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh** (2024 INSC 562) | Supreme Court of India (7-Judge Bench) | Upheld constitutional authority of State Legislatures to create sub-classifications within SC/ST categories for preferential affirmative action. |
| **Supriyo @ Supriya Chakraborty v. Union of India** (2023 INSC 920) | Supreme Court of India (5-Judge Bench) | Refused to read right to marry into Special Marriage Act for LGBTQ+ couples, leaving statutory amendments to Parliament. |
| **Justice K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India** (2017) 10 SCC 1 | Supreme Court of India (9-Judge Bench) | Privacy declared a fundamental right under Article 21; established 3-fold proportionality test. |
| **Shreya Singhal v. Union of India** (2015) 5 SCC 1 | Supreme Court of India | Struck down Section 66A of IT Act 2000 as unconstitutionally vague and overbroad under Article 19(1)(a). |
| **S.S. Lotus Case** (France v. Turkey, 1927 PCIJ Ser. A No. 10) | Permanent Court of International Justice | Sovereign states possess jurisdiction over extraterritorial collisions causing domestic harm unless prohibited by custom. |
| **Nicaragua v. United States** (1986 ICJ Reports 14) | International Court of Justice | Use of force and non-intervention are customary international law independent of UN Charter treaties; effective control test. |
| **Kulbhushan Jadhav Case** (India v. Pakistan, 2019 ICJ Reports 418) | International Court of Justice | Pakistan breached Article 36 of VCCR by denying consular access; ordered effective review and reconsideration. |
| **North Sea Continental Shelf Cases** (1969 ICJ Reports 3) | International Court of Justice | Formation of customary international law requires extensive state practice combined with *opinio juris sive necessitatis*. |
| **Reparation for Injuries Suffered in the Service of the UN** (1949 ICJ Reports 174) | International Court of Justice (Advisory Opinion) | United Nations possesses international legal personality capable of bringing international claims against states. |
| **Right of Passage over Indian Territory** (Portugal v. India, 1960 ICJ Reports 6) | International Court of Justice | Recognized existence of local or regional custom binding specifically on two states. |
