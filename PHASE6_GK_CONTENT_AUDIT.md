## 4. Practice MCQ Architecture & Pedagogical Standards

Every single one of the 700 Practice MCQs adheres strictly to the approved judicial pedagogy:
1. **Four Comprehensive Explanations:** Dedicated analysis explaining why the correct option is legally/historically sound, and why each of the three distractors is incorrect.
2. **Statutory/Historical Provision Link:** Explicit citation to bare act sections, constitutional articles, historical dates, or economic commission reports.
3. **Core Learning Objective:** Clear takeaway defining the core concept tested.
4. **Distractor Quality:** High-plausibility academic distractors reflecting typical UP PCS-J examination traps and confusion points.
