## 2. Topic Registry & Concept Architecture

All 57 topics feature structured concept notes authored for rapid conceptual clarity and judicial accuracy:
1. **Statutory Heading & Bare Act Provision**: Plain text citing India Code provisions.
2. **Key Concepts & Distinctions**: Highlighting critical judicial boundaries (e.g. Decree vs Order, Res Sub Judice vs Res Judicata, Rejection vs Return of Plaint, Set-off vs Counter-claim).
3. **Presiding Officer's Procedural Checklists**: Step-by-step judicial scrutiny rules for trial court judges.
4. **Landmark & Constitution Bench Precedents**:
   - *State of U.P. v. Nawab Hussain* (1977) — Constructive Res Judicata (Explanation IV to S. 11).
   - *Kiran Singh v. Chaman Paswan* (1954) — Inherent defect of jurisdiction vs pecuniary/territorial irregularity.
   - *T. Arivandandam v. T.V. Satyapal* (1977) — Clever drafting and rejection of vexatious plaint under Order VII Rule 11.
   - *Salem Advocate Bar Association v. Union of India* (2003, 2005) — 90-day written statement timeline is directory in exceptional cases; court fee and case management.
   - *Morgan Stanley Mutual Fund v. Kartick Das* (1994) — Guidelines for granting ex-parte ad-interim injunctions under Order XXXIX Rule 3.
   - *Manohar Lal Chopra v. Seth Hiralal* (1962) — S. 151 Inherent powers cannot override express statutory prohibitions.
5. **Active Recall Flashcards**: 3D flipping flashcards with self-testing prompts for high-speed retention.
6. **Academic SVG Diagrams**: 12 custom vector diagrams detailing procedural pipelines and statutory comparisons.
