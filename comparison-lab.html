⚠️ Statutory Notice: Section 106(2) BNS Non-Commencement Status & 1 July 2024 Transition

Pursuant to Ministry of Home Affairs Notification S.O. 850(E), Section 106(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (imposing up to 10 years imprisonment for hit-and-run fatalities without reporting) was expressly excluded from the 1 July 2024 commencement and remains NOT IN FORCE pending further central government notification. In contrast, all remaining provisions of BNS, BNSS, and BSA came into full force on 1 July 2024. Furthermore, historical UP PCS-J Previous Year Questions reflect the substantive and procedural law in force at the time of examination (IPC, CrPC, IEA) and are preserved with authentic accuracy.
