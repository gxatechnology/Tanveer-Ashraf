## 2. Daily Recommended Study Workflow

To maximize long-term retention and examination readiness, follow this 4-step daily routine:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["1. Check Dashboard & Campaign"] --> B["2. Cognitive Recall (15-30m)"]
    B --> C["3. Full Paper Mock or Practice (45-60m)"]
    C --> D["4. Revision & Misconceptions Review"]
```

1. **Morning Briefing:** Open the [Master Dashboard](index.html). The **Continue Learning** banner will immediately prompt you to resume your next campaign mission or review incomplete study units.
2. **Cognitive Warm-up:** Head to the [Legal Brain Arena](brain-arena.html). Spend 15 minutes playing **Section Sprint** for BNS recall, **Legal Crossword** for vocabulary reinforcement, or **Legal Myth Buster** to calibrate statutory exceptions.
3. **Substantive Examination Practice:** Use the [Mock Test Centre](tests.html) to simulate a 50 or 150-question examination under authentic negative marking ($1/3\text{ deduction}$).
4. **Targeted Revision:** End your session in the [Unified Revision Centre](revision.html). Work through your bookmarked PYQs and resolve items in your **Weak Knowledge Queue** and high-confidence misconceptions.

## 4. The Legal Brain Arena — All 8 Games Explained

The Brain Arena features eight distinct cognitive games, each targeted at a specific judicial skill:

### Game 1: Legal Memory Match (`memory-match.html`)
- **Objective:** Match 3D cards pairing legal maxims, statutory terms, and landmark rulings.
- **Cognitive Target:** Associative memory and rapid conceptual pairing.
- **Controls:** Tap cards to flip. Matches stay face up. Complete with minimal moves.

### Game 2: Section Sprint (`section-sprint.html`)
- **Objective:** High-speed statutory section recall against a running timer.
- **Cognitive Target:** Immediate statutory index retrieval under intense time pressure.
- **Modes:** 60-second sprint, 120-second marathon, or endless survival.

### Game 3: Legal Detective: The Case Files (`legal-detective.html`)
- **Objective:** Investigate complex criminal case scenarios, inspect forensic exhibits, interrogate suspects, and link evidence to statutory elements on an interactive corkboard.
- **Cognitive Target:** Criminal investigation analysis and statutory element mapping (BNS/BSA).
- **Cases:** 6 authored cases spanning theft vs. misappropriation, cheating vs. CBT, forgery, extortion, common intention, and degrees of culpable homicide.

### Game 4: Be the Judge: The Courtroom (`courtroom.html`)
- **Objective:** Preside over judicial bench proceedings. Hear arguments from Public Prosecutor and Defence Counsel, admit or reject exhibits, and draft a structured 3-part judicial order.
- **Cognitive Target:** Ratio decidendi formulation, application of precedents, and judicial judgment writing.
- **Cases:** 6 proceedings covering temporary injunctions, res judicata, bail applications, electronic evidence admissibility, circumstantial evidence, and natural justice.

### Game 5: Legal Crossword: The Lexicon Chamber (`crossword.html`)
- **Objective:** Solve 30 mathematically verified statutory crossword puzzles ($8\times 8$, $10\times 10$, $12\times 12$).
- **Cognitive Target:** Exact statutory vocabulary, Latin maxims, and precise terminology.
- **Controls:** Click cells to switch Across/Down. Virtual on-screen keyboard available for mobile. Solved words reveal authentic statutory definitions.

### Game 6: Legal Word Scramble: The Code Breaker (`word-scramble.html`)
- **Objective:** Decrypt scrambled statutory terms and Latin maxims using tactile letter tiles.
- **Cognitive Target:** Spelling accuracy and active recall under combo multipliers.
- **Campaign:** Unlock 5 thematic Mystery Manuscripts revealing historical judicial codices.

### Game 7: Legal Myth Buster: The Chamber of Truth (`myth-buster.html`)
- **Objective:** Adjudicate 330 verified statutory propositions as **True**, **False**, or **Depends**.
- **Cognitive Target:** Dismantling prelims traps, negative marking prevention, and mastering exceptions.
- **Unique Feature:** Animated Scales of Justice and **Confidence Calibration** (tag Low, Medium, or High confidence to uncover dangerous misconceptions).

### Game 8: Road to Judiciary: The Learning Journey (`judiciary-journey.html`)
- **Objective:** Embark on an epic 9-chapter judicial career curriculum spanning 36 connected missions across all games.
- **Cognitive Target:** Structured, comprehensive curriculum coverage from law student to judicial exam mastery.
- **Milestone:** Completing all 36 missions unlocks the *ROAD TO JUDICIARY — LEARNING JOURNEY COMPLETED* Educational Achievement Certificate.

## 5. Mock Examination Simulator Guide

1. Navigate to **Test Centre** (`tests.html`).
2. Configure your test:
   - **Mode:** Full Exam (150 questions) or Quick Practice (10 to 50 questions).
   - **Paper:** Paper I (General Knowledge) or Paper II (Law).
   - **Year:** Specific previous year (2012–2023) or All Years Combined.
   - **Scoring Rules:** Standard UP PCS-J negative marking ($1/3\text{ deduction}$) enabled by default.
3. During the exam (`exam.html`):
   - Use the **Question Palette** to jump between questions.
   - Mark challenging questions for review using the amber review flag.
   - Elapsed timer displays remaining time.
4. After submission:
   - View detailed score card, accuracy percentage, and topic performance table.
   - Review each question in `review.html` with full statutory explanations.

## 6. Smart Revision Centre & Spaced Repetition

Your mistakes are your greatest learning opportunities:
- When you answer incorrectly in mock exams or identify misconceptions in Legal Myth Buster, items automatically enter your **Weak Knowledge Queue**.
- In the [Unified Revision Centre](revision.html), use the filter tabs:
  - **All Items:** Everything needing revision.
  - **Bookmarked PYQs:** Questions you starred while browsing the library.
  - **Arena Errors & Misconceptions:** Game mistakes and high-confidence misconceptions.
- Once you have thoroughly reviewed an item, click **Mark Resolved** to graduate it from the queue.

## 7. Data Safety: Backing Up Your Progress

Because your study records stay on your local device:
1. Navigate to **Results & Progress** (`results.html`).
2. Scroll to the **Unified Data Backup & Recovery** section.
3. Click **Export Complete Backup**. A timestamped `.json` file (`tanveer-pcsj-unified-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`) will download to your device.
4. To resume your progress on another device (e.g. tablet or laptop), open the same page, select **Import Backup**, and choose your backup file.
5. All test history, saved questions, read markers, game XP, achievements, and campaign missions will be restored immediately.
