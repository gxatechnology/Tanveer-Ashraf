## 2. Classification Breakdown

### A. Substantive Sections (48 Entries)
1. **Section 2(2) — Decree**: Essential elements (formal expression, conclusive adjudication, rights of parties, suit). Preliminary vs Final Decrees.
2. **Section 2(9) — Judgment**: Definition and distinction from Decree.
3. **Section 2(12) — Mesne Profits**: Profits wrongfully received, interest thereon (not exceeding 6%), excluding improvements.
4. **Section 2(14) — Order**: Formal expression of civil court decision not being a decree.
5. **Section 9 — Courts to try all civil suits unless barred**: Presumption of jurisdiction; Express and Implied bars.
6. **Section 10 — Res Sub Judice (Stay of Suit)**: Conditions for stay; pending suit in concurrent jurisdiction; interlocutory orders not barred.
7. **Section 11 — Res Judicata**: Conclusive bar; 8 Explanations (Explanation IV Constructive Res Judicata, Explanation VIII Limited Jurisdiction).
8. **Section 12 — Bar to further suit**: Bar under Order II Rule 2, Order IX Rule 9, Order XXII Rule 9, Order XXIII Rule 1.
9. **Section 15 — Court in which suits to be instituted**: Court of lowest grade competent to try it.
10. **Section 16 — Suits to be instituted where subject-matter situate**: Immovable property recovery, partition, mortgage, torts to immovable property.
11. **Section 17 — Suits for immovable property situate within jurisdiction of different courts**: Any court within whose local limits any portion is situate.
12. **Section 18 — Place of institution where local limits of jurisdiction of courts are uncertain**: Recording statement of uncertainty.
13. **Section 19 — Suits for wrongs to person or movables**: Defendant resides/carries on business OR where the wrong was done (Plaintiff's choice).
14. **Section 20 — Other suits to be instituted where defendants reside or cause of action arises**: Residence of all defendants, or leave of court, or cause of action wholly or in part.
15. **Section 21 — Objections to jurisdiction**: Three conditions (earliest opportunity, before issue settlement, failure of justice).
16. **Section 21A — Bar on suit to set aside decree on objection as to place of suing**: Complete bar to fresh suits.
17. **Section 26 — Institution of suits**: Presentation of plaint with affidavit proving facts.
18. **Section 27 — Summons to defendants**: Within 30 days from the date of institution.
19. **Section 34 — Interest**: Pre-suit, pendente lite (court discretion), and post-decree interest (not exceeding 6% p.a., commercial exception).
20. **Section 35 — Costs**: Costs to follow the event; recording reasons if not awarded.
21. **Section 35A — Compensatory costs in respect of false or vexatious claims or defences**: Statutory cap of ₹3,000/- or pecuniary limit.
22. **Section 35B — Costs for causing delay**: Mandatory pre-condition to further step in litigation.
23. **Section 38 — Court by which decree may be executed**: Court passing the decree or court to which sent.
24. **Section 39 — Transfer of decree**: Grounds for transfer of execution.
25. **Section 46 — Precepts**: Issue of precept to attach property in another court (maximum 2 months without extension).
26. **Section 47 — Questions to be determined by Court executing decree**: Exclusive jurisdiction; bar on separate suit for execution, discharge, satisfaction.
27. **Section 51 — Powers of Court to enforce execution**: Delivery of property, attachment/sale, arrest/detention, receiver.
28. **Section 60 — Property liable to attachment and sale in execution of decree**: Non-attachable items (tools of artisans, agricultural implements, 1/3rd salary in maintenance, ₹1,000 + 2/3rd in other decrees).
29. **Section 75 — Power of court to issue commissions**: 7 statutory purposes.
30. **Section 79 — Suits by or against Government**: Union of India or State of Uttar Pradesh.
31. **Section 80 — Notice to Government / Public Officer**: 2 months mandatory notice; Section 80(2) leave for urgent interim relief.
32. **Section 82 — Execution of decree against Government**: 3 months period before execution can issue.
33. **Section 88 — Where interpleader suit may be instituted**: Two or more persons claiming adversely; plaintiff claiming only costs.
34. **Section 89 — Settlement of disputes outside the Court**: ADR mechanisms (Arbitration, Conciliation, Judicial Settlement, Lok Adalat, Mediation).
35. **Section 94 — Supplemental proceedings**: Arrest, security, temporary injunction, receiver to prevent justice defeated.
36. **Section 95 — Compensation for obtaining arrest, attachment or injunction on insufficient grounds**: Cap of ₹50,000/-.
37. **Section 96 — Appeal from original decree**: Statutory right; First appeal on law and facts; Bar on consent decrees (S. 96(3)).
38. **Section 100 — Second appeal**: Restricted exclusively to a "Substantial Question of Law"; mandatory framing of question by High Court.
39. **Section 102 — No second appeal in certain suits**: Suits for recovery of money not exceeding ₹25,000/-.
40. **Section 104 — Orders from which appeal lies**: Statutory list of appealable orders.
41. **Section 113 — Reference to High Court**: Subordinate court's reference on question of constitutional or statutory validity.
42. **Section 114 — Review**: Decree or order from which appeal allowed but not preferred, or no appeal allowed.
43. **Section 115 — Revision**: High Court's superintendence; non-appealable orders; jurisdictional error; proviso (final disposal test).
44. **Section 144 — Application for restitution**: Restitution upon variation or reversal of decree; bar on separate suit.
45. **Section 148 — Enlargement of time**: Judicial discretion; capped at total 30 days.
46. **Section 148A — Right to lodge a caveat**: 90-day validity; notice to caveator; ex-parte protection.
47. **Section 151 — Saving of inherent powers of Court**: Abuse of process & ends of justice; residual power cannot contravene express provisions.
48. **Section 152 — Amendment of judgments, decrees or orders**: Clerical or arithmetical mistakes arising from accidental slip or omission.

### B. Procedural Orders & Rules (35 Entries)
1. **Order I Rule 1 & Rule 3 — Joinder of Plaintiffs & Defendants**: Common right to relief and common question of law/fact.
2. **Order I Rule 8 — Representative Suit**: One person suing/defending on behalf of all; court permission, public notice.
3. **Order I Rule 9 — Misjoinder and non-joinder**: Suit not defeated except for non-joinder of necessary party.
4. **Order I Rule 10 — Court may strike out or add parties**: Bona fide mistake; proper and necessary parties.
5. **Order II Rule 2 — Suit to include the whole claim**: Splitting of claims barred; omission without court leave bars fresh suit.
6. **Order V Rule 1 — Summons**: Issue of summons within 30 days.
7. **Order V Rule 9 — Delivery of summons by Court**: Registered post, speed post, courier, email.
8. **Order V Rule 20 — Substituted service**: Affixation and newspaper publication when defendant keeps out of the way.
9. **Order VI Rule 16 — Striking out pleadings**: Unnecessary, scandalous, frivolous or vexatious.
10. **Order VI Rule 17 — Amendment of pleadings**: Proviso bar after commencement of trial unless due diligence proved.
11. **Order VII Rule 10 — Return of plaint**: Plaint presented to court lacking jurisdiction.
12. **Order VII Rule 11 — Rejection of plaint**: 6 grounds (no cause of action, undervaluation, insufficient stamp, barred by law, duplicate defect, Rule 9 non-compliance).
13. **Order VIII Rule 1 — Written statement**: 30-day timeline; extension up to 90 days (Salem Advocate Bar Ass'n).
14. **Order VIII Rule 6 — Set-off**: Ascertained sum, legally recoverable, both parties same character, within pecuniary jurisdiction.
15. **Order VIII Rule 6A — Counter-claim**: Cause of action arising before or after filing up to defendant delivering defence.
16. **Order IX Rule 6 — Procedure when only plaintiff appears**: Ex-parte proceeding when summons duly served.
17. **Order IX Rule 8 — Procedure where defendant only appears**: Dismissal of suit for plaintiff's default.
18. **Order IX Rule 9 — Decree against plaintiff by default bars fresh suit**: Remedy of application for restoration.
19. **Order IX Rule 13 — Setting aside ex parte decree against defendant**: Two grounds (summons not duly served, sufficient cause).
20. **Order XI Rule 1 — Discovery by interrogatories**: Leave of court; questions of fact relevant to matter in question.
21. **Order XII Rule 6 — Judgment on admissions**: Discretionary judgment on admissions in pleadings or otherwise.
22. **Order XIV Rule 1 — Framing of issues**: Issues of fact and issues of law; first hearing of suit.
23. **Order XIV Rule 2 — Court to pronounce judgment on all issues**: Preliminary issue of law only on jurisdiction or statutory bar.
24. **Order XVI Rule 1 — List of witnesses**: Filing within 15 days of issue settlement.
25. **Order XVII Rule 1 — Court may grant time and adjourn hearing**: Cap of 3 adjournments during hearing of suit.
26. **Order XVIII Rule 4 — Examination-in-chief on affidavit**: Cross-examination before court or commissioner.
27. **Order XX Rule 1 — Judgment when pronounced**: Within 30 days, maximum 60 days under exceptional reasons.
28. **Order XXI Rule 11 — Oral application for arrest; Written application**: Modes of execution applications.
29. **Order XXI Rule 46 — Attachment of debt, share and other property (Garnishee Order)**: Prohibition notice to third party.
30. **Order XXVI Rule 9 — Commissions to make local investigations**: Elucidating matter in dispute, market value.
31. **Order XXXII Rule 1 & Rule 3 — Minor suits**: Next friend for minor plaintiff; Guardian ad litem for minor defendant.
32. **Order XXXIII Rule 1 — Suits by indigent persons**: Incapable of paying court fee; exempt from court fee, not decree.
33. **Order XXXVII Rule 1 & 2 — Summary procedure**: Bills of exchange, promissory notes, liquidated contractual debts.
34. **Order XXXIX Rule 1 & Rule 2 — Temporary injunctions**: Tri-partite test: prima facie case, irreparable injury, balance of convenience.
35. **Order XLI Rule 27 — Production of additional evidence in Appellate Court**: Three strict conditions.
