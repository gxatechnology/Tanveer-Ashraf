## 1. Overview & Pedagogical Purpose

The Social Legislation Statutory Explorer provides rapid, high-yield statutory lookup tailored specifically for the Uttar Pradesh Judicial Services Preliminary Examination. Every single entry contains:
- **Statutory Reference:** Exact Section and sub-section.
- **Title & Core Rule:** Plain English summary of the legal mandate.
- **Conditions & Prerequisites:** Statutory requirements for invocation.
- **Exceptions & Provisos:** Statutory caveats and non-applicability.
- **Penalties & Punishment:** Imprisonment terms, mandatory minimums, fines, and forfeiture.
- **UP PCS-J Exam Tips:** High-frequency trap warnings, statutory cross-references, and exam tricks.
- **India Code Reference:** Authoritative digital Bare Act citation.

## 3. High-Yield Statutory Traps Summary

1. **40% Benchmark Disability Trap:** Essential for affirmative action (reservations under Sections 32 and 34), but *Vikas Kumar (2021)* establishes that reasonable accommodation under Section 3 applies to **all** disabled persons regardless of percentage.
2. **Maintenance Cap Trap:** The Central Act 56 of 2007 contains an express statutory ceiling of **maximum ₹10,000 per month** under Section 9(2). In preliminary questions, candidates often mistakenly choose 'unlimited' or 'one-third salary'.
3. **Bar of Advocates Trap:** Section 17 of the Senior Citizens Act contains an **absolute bar** on legal practitioners representing parties before the Tribunal.
4. **Customary Presents List Trap:** Under Dowry Prohibition Section 3(2), presents given at marriage are NOT dowry only if made **without demand** and recorded in a list signed by both bride and groom conforming to 1985 Rules.
5. **Female Respondents under PWDVA:** The Supreme Court in *Hiral P. Harsora (2016)* struck down 'adult male' in Section 2(q); mother-in-law, sister-in-law, and female relatives CAN be arrayed as respondents.
6. **Shared Household Ownership Trap:** *Satish Chander Ahuja (2021)* confirmed the wife can claim residence in joint family property or in-laws' house where she lived in a domestic relationship, even if the husband has no legal title.
7. **PCPNDT Cognizance Trap:** Police cannot register a regular FIR on private complaint. Under Section 28, cognizance can only be taken on the formal complaint of the **Appropriate Authority**.
8. **MTP Consent Trap:** For an adult woman (18+), her consent **alone** is required under Section 3(4)(a). Husband's or parent's consent is completely irrelevant in law.
9. **POCSO Reverse Burden Trap:** Under Sections 29 and 30, the court **shall presume** guilt once prosecution proves foundational acts; this is a statutory mandate, not a discretionary inference (*may presume*).
